About Evergreen

Purpose built for long-term value.

Evergreen Holdings Ltd is a private company limited by shares, incorporated on 30 June 2024 and headquartered in Kimihurura, Kigali.

As a holding company, Evergreen provides strategic oversight and governance to Agribloom Farms Ltd and Pure Harvest Farms Ltd. Together, they produce and export avocados, vegetables and strawberries.

Vision

A trusted international agribusiness group.

Our produce, systems and ethics will set a benchmark for reliability, sustainability and long-term value across global markets.

Mission

High-quality food at scale.

We responsibly steward land, people and capital while demonstrating what productive, sustainable and commercially scalable agriculture can achieve in Rwanda.
