About Evergreen

Purpose built for long-term value.

Evergreen Holdings Ltd is a private company limited by shares, incorporated on 30 June 2024 and headquartered in Kimihurura, Kigali.

We build and grow world-class, technology-driven agribusinesses.

As a holding company, Evergreen provides strategic oversight and governance to Agribloom Farms Ltd and Pure Harvest Farms Ltd. Together, they produce and export avocados, vegetables and strawberries.

Our approach combines disciplined operations, modern agricultural systems and uncompromising quality standards—from water and energy to healthy soils and post-harvest infrastructure.

Vision

A trusted international agribusiness group.

Our produce, systems and ethics set a benchmark for reliability, sustainability and long-term value.

Mission

High-quality food at scale.

We responsibly steward land, people and capital while demonstrating what scalable agriculture can achieve in Rwanda.
